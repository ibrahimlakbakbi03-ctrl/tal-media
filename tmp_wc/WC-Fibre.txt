Late 1990s: telecom companies raced to lay fibre-optic cable across America. By 2001, only about 5% of it was in use. Some went bankrupt and investors lost fortunes. But the cables stayed, and years later they carried the internet you use today. The technology won. The investors who paid for it lost.
