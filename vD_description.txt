Ray Dalio says America has three years before a debt crisis. He first said it in March 2025, and said it again in October 2026. So is he right, or is the clock just moving with him? We checked his 5 warning signs against the data and against history: Japan, the US after 1946, the UK in 2022 and Greece in 2010.

The verdict: Dalio's math is right. His clock is a guess. And for a country that prints its own money, a debt crisis usually doesn't look like a crash. It looks like your money buying less.

Is Dalio right, early, or wrong? Tell us in the comments.
