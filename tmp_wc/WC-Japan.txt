Japan's government owes about twice its whole economy, more than any other rich country. Why no collapse? It borrows in yen, money it can print. And the Bank of Japan holds about 47% of its government bonds; foreigners hold under 8%. The twist: someone still paid. For about 30 years, Japanese savers earned almost nothing.
